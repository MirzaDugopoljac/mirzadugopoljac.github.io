01 / Position

Landscape architecture can do more than fill space.

I want to understand why a place works the way it does — then make a meaningful change to that system.

My work moves between spatial design, planning, planting, GIS and visualization. The goal is not simply to produce an attractive drawing, but to find the move that makes the site clearer, more useful and more memorable.

How I see a site As layers of movement, ecology, behavior, material and time.
What I want the work to do Turn existing conditions into opportunities people can actually experience.
01 / OBSERVE

Read before drawing.

Understand terrain, movement, climate, vegetation, people and constraints before deciding what the place needs.

02 / REFRAME

Find the leverage point.

A design move matters when it changes several things at once — circulation, comfort, identity, ecology or use.

03 / TRANSFORM

Make the idea spatial.

Translate the strategy into paths, planting, edges, structures, materials and atmospheres people can inhabit.

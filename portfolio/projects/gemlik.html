01 / Coastal Landscape

Proje 5 — Gemlik

A coastal landscape where ecology, memory and everyday life meet the Sea of Marmara.

Type Coastal Landscape
Context Gemlik, Bursa · 2026
Focus Coastal ecology · public space · exposure

Project story

Developed for Landscape Project 5 at Bursa Uludağ University, Gemlik explores exposure to wind, waves, salinity, pollution and human activity. The sea cucumber concept translates these pressures into a public landscape: cultivation pools, environmental information stations, a coastal memory workshop and a continuous ecology route connect recreation with awareness of the coast. The final jury submission is dated 26 June 2026.

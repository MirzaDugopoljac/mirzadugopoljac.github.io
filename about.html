I’m interested in

I’m Mirza Dugopoljac, a Landscape Architecture student working across spatial design, planning, visualization and GIS. I’m drawn to projects where design can respond to real conditions — movement, ecology, behavior, climate, material and time — rather than simply decorate a site.

Current direction Landscape design, spatial analysis and visualization.
Based in Bursa, Türkiye
01 / Position

For me, landscape architecture starts with understanding.

A good intervention should feel like it belongs there — but also make the place work differently.

I’m most interested in the moment when analysis turns into a spatial decision: when a path, planting system, edge, structure or open space begins to solve more than one problem at once.

What I look for Patterns, constraints and opportunities hidden inside the site.
What I want to improve The relationship between people, place, ecology and everyday use.

03 / Approach

I’m trying to build a practice that can move from analysis to experience.

01 / READ

Understand the site.

Terrain, vegetation, circulation, users, climate and existing patterns come before formal decisions.

02 / REFRAME

Find the real problem.

A useful design question often appears after the first obvious problem has been questioned.

03 / TEST

Compare alternatives.

Plans, sections, models, GIS and visualization help me test whether an idea actually works spatially.

04 / TRANSFORM

Make the change visible.

The final move should improve use, experience, identity or ecological performance — ideally several at once.

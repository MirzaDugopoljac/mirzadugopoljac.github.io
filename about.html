I’m interested in why places work the way they do — and what a landscape architect can change without erasing what already makes the site specific.

Current direction Landscape design, spatial analysis and visualization.
Based in Bursa, Türkiye
My position

Landscape is a relationship, not a layer added at the end.

The work gets interesting when ecology, movement, behavior, material and form begin to support each other.

01 / Position

A good intervention should belong to the place — and still change how it works.

I’m most interested in the moment when analysis becomes a spatial decision.

A path, planting system, edge, structure or open space becomes valuable when it solves more than one problem and improves the relationship between people, ecology and everyday use.

What I look forPatterns, constraints and opportunities hidden inside the site.
What I’m building towardA practice comfortable with terrain, drawings, analysis and visual communication.
